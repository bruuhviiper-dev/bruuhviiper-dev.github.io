Privacy Policy

App Smart Tools (com.smarttoolbox.tools) · Phantom Tecnologia · Updated September 15, 2026.

This Policy describes how the Smart Tools app handles information. By using the app, you agree to the practices described here. We collect as little data as possible, and the tools run on your device.

1. Data the app does NOT collect

The app requires no sign-up or login and does not collect personal data on servers. Your settings (theme, units) stay only on your device. Most tools work offline.

2. Camera (Magnifier, Mirror, QR Scanner)

The camera is used only while you use these tools, to show a live image or read a code. The image is processed on your device and is never recorded or uploaded.

3. Microphone (Sound Meter)

The microphone measures the ambient noise level (decibels) in real time. Audio is analyzed on your device and is not recorded, saved or sent anywhere.

4. Location (Qibla, Speedometer)

Location is used only on your device to compute the Qibla direction and your GPS speed. Your location is not sent to us or to any server.

5. Sensors

Compass, level, protractor, metal detector and vibration tools use the phone's motion and magnetic sensors, processed locally.

6. Data export (Pro)

If you record and export a CSV (e.g. from the Sound Meter), the file is created on your device and shared only if you choose to share it.

7. Ads (Google AdMob)

The free app shows ads via Google AdMob. Google and partners may use device identifiers (such as the Advertising ID) and usage data.

Learn more: Google Policy and how AdMob uses data. Smart Tools Pro removes ads.

8. In-app purchases

Purchases (Pro monthly, yearly or lifetime) are processed by Google Play. We have no access to your payment data.

9. Permissions summary

  • Internet: to show ads.
  • Camera: magnifier, mirror, QR scanner.
  • Microphone: sound meter only.
  • Location: Qibla and speedometer.

10. Audience

A utility app suitable for a general audience. It is not directed to children.

11. Changes

We may update this Policy. The last update date is shown at the top.

12. Contact
